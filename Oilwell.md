# Oil Well Production Optimization Using Machine Learning

This project demonstrates how **machine learning** can be used to optimize **oil well production** by predicting flow rates and identifying key operational factors such as **pressure**, **temperature**, **pump speed**, and **oil viscosity**. This approach can be applied by oil companies like **Sonatrach** to improve their operations.

## How Machine Learning Can Help Increase Oil Production

### 1. What Does Machine Learning Do?
Machine learning (ML) helps a computer learn from **historical data** and **make predictions**. In oil production, this means using data from the oil wells to understand which factors affect the amount of oil being produced, and how to optimize those factors for **maximum production**.

### 2. How This Project Helps
In this project, I applied **Random Forest** and **Gradient Boosting Machine (GBM)** models to predict **oil flow rates** based on different operational factors:
- **Pressure**
- **Temperature**
- **Pump Speed**
- **Oil Viscosity**

The models were able to predict flow rates with **high accuracy**, and provided insights into which factors had the biggest impact on production.

### 3. Increasing Oil Production with AI
Here’s how companies like Sonatrach can use machine learning to **optimize production**:

#### Step 1: Monitor and Collect Data
Collect real-time data from the wells, including:
- Well **pressure**
- **Temperature** of the oil
- **Pump speed**
- **Oil viscosity**

This data is fed into the **machine learning model**.

#### Step 2: Analyze the Data
The machine learning model analyzes the data and **predicts** oil flow rates based on the different operational factors. It helps identify which settings produce the **most oil**.

#### Step 3: Optimize Operations
Using insights from the model, the company can adjust the following to maximize production:
- **Increase well pressure** if the model shows that higher pressure produces more oil.
- Reduce **oil viscosity** by adjusting temperature or adding chemicals, which improves the flow rate.
- Adjust **pump speed** to ensure optimal performance without overuse or damaging equipment.

#### Step 4: Continuously Improve
As the AI system collects more data, it keeps learning and improving its predictions. It can automatically suggest the **best operational settings** for maximizing oil production.

## Results from the Project

### Model Performance:
- **GBM RMSE**: 5.32 (very accurate predictions)
- **Random Forest RMSE**: 7.72 (still very accurate)

### Optimal Parameter Settings:
- **Pressure**: 3500 psi
- **Temperature**: 140°F
- **Pump Speed**: 104 rpm
- **Oil Viscosity**: 17 centipoise
- **Predicted Flow Rate**: 190 barrels/day

### Key Factors for Maximizing Oil Production:
- **Pressure** is the most important factor, followed by **oil viscosity** and **temperature**. 
- **Pump speed** has a lesser effect, but still plays a role in production efficiency.

## Conclusion:
By using **machine learning** models like **Random Forest** and **GBM**, oil companies can **predict** the best operational settings and make data-driven decisions to **increase oil production**. This approach provides **actionable insights** that can optimize well operations, improve efficiency, and maximize output.
